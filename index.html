Before you begin

Two different jobs in one design decision

This tool gives formative feedback on your proposed engineering design requirements and criteria. It will point out areas worth discussing and ask questions that can help your team revise the work yourselves.

Requirement

A condition an acceptable design must satisfy. Think pass/fail.

Course format: The design must...

Criterion

A characteristic used to compare acceptable design alternatives. Think better/worse.

Course format: The design shall...

A characteristic can sometimes be both: a requirement may set the minimum acceptable threshold while a criterion distinguishes among designs that meet it.
